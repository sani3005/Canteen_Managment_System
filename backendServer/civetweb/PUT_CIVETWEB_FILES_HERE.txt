Download CivetWeb from https://github.com/civetweb/civetweb/releases
(or "Code > Download ZIP"), then copy these into THIS folder:

  include/civetweb.h        ->  civetweb/civetweb.h
  src/civetweb.c            ->  civetweb/civetweb.c
  src/*.inl                 ->  civetweb/   (all the .inl files, if your version has them)

Then you can delete this text file.
